# Early-pole numerical checkpoint: what is resolved and what is not

**Do not use these cold spectra as converged physical outputs or pass them to a cosmological transfer solver.** This checkpoint summarizes the limited numerical evidence from the open independent work. It does not verify the production model, supply a live photon occupation, or treat a CI state that requires action as a passing check.

| Question | Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Does the retained cold band integrate reliably on a fixed D4 state? | [Complete native D1/D2/D4 ladder](INDEPENDENT-EARLY-POLE-ENDPOINT-FINE-D4-COLD-CONTINUUM-LADDER-2026-11-28.md): both D4 integrations have all 108 exact panels and agree to 6.93e−14 on the nine integrated coefficients. | Accept that fixed-state numerical integration; it is not a spatial error estimate. |
| Does the result converge when the charged grid is refined? | [D4 subband enclosure](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_FINE_D4_COLD_SUBBAND_ENCLOSURE.md) and [normalized profile comparison](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_FINE_CHARGE_COLD_NORMALIZED_CONTRAST.md): D4 gross forward number is 5.872% of D2 over the same band, all five tested subbands have smaller absolute gross contributions, and normalized forward mass cannot have compatible D2/D4 shapes (minimum TV about 1.98%). | Do not claim spatial convergence, even though both D4 quadratures agree closely. |
| Can the held sign reversal be assigned uniformly across cold momentum? | [Constant-occupation thresholds](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_COLD_NEUTRAL_OCCUPATION_CORRIDORS.md) exclude simultaneous full and upper-band reversal for any one constant occupation; a [small smooth ramp](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_COLD_AFFINE_OCCUPATION.md) makes both possible on both admitted meshes. | The sign depends on the held profile. Neither construction determines a live photon occupation. |
| What changes the gap and the actual terminal history? | [Count-only Hartree gap substitutions](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_CHARGE_COLD_COUNT_GAP_LADDER.md), [matched times](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_CHARGE_COLD_COUNT_HISTORY_BUDGET.md), [complete crossed terminal runs](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_CROSSED_CHARGE_TERMINAL_HISTORY_RESULT.md), and [controlled charge remap](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_CHARGE_GRID_DONOR_TERMINAL.md). | On this .04 restart, count largely controls the gap shifts; changing the charged chart and state dominates the late cold difference. Grid and its chosen representation dominate a second comparison with fixed scalar seed. Neither statement is unique physical attribution. |
| Could a time-step artifact explain the symmetric shape response? | [Same-start 2048/4096 refinement](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_SYMMETRIC_CHARGE_TIME_REFINEMENT.md): all 16 saved response signs and all three observed odd sign-reversal brackets are preserved; terminal even and odd magnitudes agree within .03%. | Do not assign the observed response to time-step size on these two clocks. This does not bound other shapes or spatial error. |
| Does the finite endpoint respond the same way at different perturbation amplitudes? | [Symmetric half-amplitude reruns](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_SYMMETRIC_CHARGE_HALF_AMPLITUDE.md): the effect odd in perturbation sign is 1.031 times the linearly rescaled result; the even effect is 0.9987 times the quadratically rescaled result. On the upper run the terminal cold count switches from below nominal to above nominal; [the cutoff audit](EARLY_POLE_SYMMETRIC_CHARGE_CUTOFF_AUDIT.md) certifies the sign pattern for any threshold on the same chart from q=9.98177e−6 to 25, conditional on the saved cell masses. | Do not infer a sign that holds at every amplitude, a Taylor coefficient, or stability from two finite perturbation amplitudes. |
| Does higher count imply higher pressure or energy? | [Fixed-mass kinetic pressure](EARLY_POLE_SYMMETRIC_CHARGE_PRESSURE.md) and [native energy balance](EARLY_POLE_SYMMETRIC_CHARGE_TERMINAL_ENERGY.md): the half-upper state has more scalar particles, lower scalar kinetic pressure and higher scalar energy. The [mean-field pressure](EARLY_POLE_SYMMETRIC_CHARGE_HARTREE_PRESSURE.md) reverses the sign of the model scalar pressure. Charged energies and the mean-field correction are needed to reconcile the lower corrected total with the saved expansion trace. | Do not infer pressure from total count or the sign of total energy from the scalar sector; pressure stability is conditional on the stated reconstruction constraints. |
| Is the independent two-bath FD observable stable under every numerical alteration? | [Endpoint entropy gain comparison](FD_PORTAL_SPLINE_ENTROPY_ENDPOINT_GAIN.md) and [local EOS knot check](FD_PORTAL_ENTROPY_PCHIP_KNOT_CROSSING.md). | Selected endpoint gains are nearly unchanged, but local signs depend on the interpolation derivative. [Reoptimized heat-capacity thresholds](FD_PORTAL_HEAT_CAPACITY_SLOPE_THRESHOLD.md) show the slope crossing a nearly fixed threshold between the two sides of the knot; [plane geometry](FD_PORTAL_ENTROPY_PLANE_ROTATION_THRESHOLD.md) distinguishes initial from instantaneous energy restrictions. This is restricted FD dynamics, not a physical stability claim or full plasma source. |
| Can new cold quadratures be run more efficiently? | [Disjoint exact native shards](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_NATIVE_COLD_SHARDS.md), [transverse factorization](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_TRANSVERSE_FACTORIZED_SPINOR.md) [complete optional D8 panel replay](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_FACTOR_TRANSVERSE_NATIVE_D8_PANEL.md), and [real-Pauli fine panel](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_REAL_PAULI_NATIVE_D8_FINE_PANEL.md). In one exact fine panel the nine coefficients matched stock within 3.22e−15, and observed execution fell from about 120 s for the optional complex path to 62 s for the optional real path. | Use optional exact-tagged workloads in future. Do not switch a running stock ledger or mix stock and alternate rows. Measured point speed is not a full-band runtime guarantee. |

## Next decision gate

The [D8 central-band protocol](EARLY_POLE_ENDPOINT_FINE_D8_CENTRAL_COLD_BAND_PROTOCOL.md) restricts the independent next comparison to q=[3e−6,5e−6], a band containing more than 80% of D2 and D4 gross forward weight by conservative enclosures. It is narrower than the earlier full held band; do not compare those two totals as if their integration limits matched. Require both complete D8 quadratures and their exact topology and numerical checks before comparing D8 to the D4 common-band interval. Report magnitude and held conditional net signs separately, or report unresolved signs. Use both observed mesh coefficients and preserve unresolved signs when their coefficient box includes zero; that box is not a continuum error bound. No partial native ledger is a source estimate.
